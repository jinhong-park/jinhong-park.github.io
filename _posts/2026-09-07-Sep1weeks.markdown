**Title:** Two-magnon response from light scattering in altermagnets
**ID:** [2609.09287v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09287)
**Authors:** Shuyi Li, Lexu Zhao, Chun-Jing Jia
**Date:** 09/10 entry (arXiv 8 Sep 2026)

---

**why this pick?**

This week's collection (~250 entries) has **no altermagnetism × band-topology entry at all** — the three-week Lieb-lattice arc (08/17 → 08/24 → 08/31) has no sequel this week — so the pick shifts to the second axis of your research program: **the magnetic excitation sector of altermagnets**. 2609.09287 is the only entry that makes **altermagnetism, magnons, and theory all core**: it takes the canonical **2D d-wave altermagnet** (same model family as the 08/24 pick 2608.23442 and the 12cl-b9jj magnon-geometry paper) and computes **finite-momentum two-magnon light-scattering intensities** with linear spin-wave theory plus leading **1/S quantum corrections**. The headline result is a concrete, momentum-selective altermagnetic fingerprint: the characteristic magnon splitting of d-wave altermagnets shifts the **upper edge of the two-magnon continuum** and the K-point degenerate two-magnon resonance to higher energy by an amount **linear in the exchange anisotropy δJ₂**, while the zone-center response is unchanged — and interactions add a low-energy two-peak structure with no linear-spin-wave counterpart. It directly answers an open problem stated in the abstract — *identifying the spectroscopic fingerprints of altermagnetism* — and motivates momentum-resolved RESX as the experiment.

**3. Physical insights from this paper**

- **The altermagnetic fingerprint is momentum-selective**: the zone center (Γ) shows *no* altermagnetic signature in the two-magnon scattering intensity, so conventional zone-center Raman cannot distinguish d-wave altermagnets from ordinary AFMs — the signature lives at **X = (π, 0)** (continuum-edge shift linear in δJ₂) and **K = (π/2, π/2)** (degeneracy splitting of the two-magnon resonance). This is a sharp, falsifiable diagnostic that complements last week's companion 12cl-b9jj (optical protocol via magnon chirality) and 2609.02008 (π-periodic third-order thermal Hall).
- **Interactions are not a correction — they create structure**: beyond LSWT, magnon–magnon interactions both protect the high-energy reconstruction (it survives weight redistribution) and generate a low-energy double-peak that *does not exist* in the linear theory — the same "interactions add physics" theme as the 08/24 pick (2608.23442, ED-verified co-existence phase).
- **Reading together = one package**: 08/24 pick (interaction-driven altermagnet × QSH co-existence on the Lieb lattice) → 12cl-b9jj (magnon quantum geometry of d-wave altermagnets) → **this** (two-magnon spectroscopy + 1/S corrections) → 2609.04883 (which point groups can be d-/g-/i-wave and why). That is the week's most coherent thread across your altermagnet + magnon + theoretical interests.

**TL;DR**

> In a 2D d-wave altermagnet, finite-momentum two-magnon light scattering carries a fingerprint of altermagnetic order that is invisible at the zone center: the altermagnetic exchange anisotropy δJ₂ shifts the two-magnon continuum edge at X and splits the degenerate K-point two-magnon resonance linearly, with interactions adding a robust double-peak structure. Linear spin-wave + 1/S quantum corrections, abstract-checked, motivated for momentum-resolved RESX — the week's only entry with altermagnet, magnon, and theoretical all core, and the spectroscopic capstone on the d-wave altermagnet model family the past three weeks' picks have been developing.
